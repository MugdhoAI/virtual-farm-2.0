The Reality

Agricultural decisions happen in the field—with real costs attached.

  • A pilot trains in a simulator before their first flight.
  • A surgeon practices on models before their first operation.
  • An engineer runs digital stress tests before building the first prototype.

Boro rice farming involves decisions about land, seed, fertilizer, labor, irrigation, and when to sell or store a harvest.

Virtual Farm is being developed as a way to explore how those decisions may affect a season. Its production model is not available until the required agricultural and economic inputs can be verified.

Reliable decision support requires transparent methods and traceable evidence for every value used.

That evidence is a prerequisite, not a placeholder.

A useful simulator must make every modeled input traceable to evidence.

The Product

A concept for a Boro rice decision simulator.

The planned simulator follows six Boro rice decisions from season setup through a financial outcome and diagnostic. The production model is currently blocked because required source-backed values are not available.

The Process

How the planned simulation is structured.

  1. 01

    Review Your Conditions

    The planned flow begins with farm conditions such as land size. Verified production starting conditions are not yet available.

  2. 02

    Make Six Key Decisions

    The planned choices are farm size, seed quality, fertilizer, labor or automation, irrigation, and whether to sell or store. Validated costs, yield effects, and risks remain to be sourced.

  3. 03

    The Season Runs

    Weather and market behavior can only be modeled after their probabilities and effects are supported by production data. Those inputs are not currently available.

  4. 04

    Your Result

    The intended result is a BDT profit or loss. No production result can be calculated until required model inputs are verified.

  5. 05

    The Diagnostic

    The planned diagnostic will explain modeled contributions. Causal comparisons and an optimal path are not yet implemented as a production feature.

    Data Requirements

    Production inputs are not yet verified.

    The sources below are potential or intended sources to investigate—not evidence that the required data has been obtained. No production agricultural or economic values are currently verified in the model.

    BBS

    Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics

    Potential source

    Yield statistics

    Evidence needed

    A cited, applicable Boro yield dataset with units, geography, season, and methodology.

    DAE

    Dept. of Agricultural Extension

    Potential source

    Input costs

    Evidence needed

    Source records for seed, fertilizer, labor, machinery, and irrigation costs with date and geography.

    FPMU

    Food Planning & Monitoring Unit

    Potential source

    Paddy prices

    Evidence needed

    Producer-side paddy prices and traceable time-series evidence for any storage price movement.

    BWDB

    Bangladesh Water Development Board

    Potential source

    Weather risks

    Evidence needed

    Applicable Boro-season weather observations, event probabilities, and sourced yield effects.

    BARI

    Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute

    Potential source

    Crop effects

    Evidence needed

    Sourced agronomic evidence for any modeled input effect, soil degradation, or crop risk.

    Field

    Direct Farmer Interviews

    Potential source

    Ground-truth evidence

    Evidence needed

    Documented farmer research with consent, sampling method, location, season, and verified records.

    Source citations and update cadence will be documented if and when qualifying evidence is obtained.
